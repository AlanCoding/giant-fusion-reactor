# Catalytic Deuterium Breeder

[← Fuel cycle](README.md) · [Study navigation](../README.md)

This candidate pathway uses recyclable CNO-family nuclei to make deuterium from ordinary hydrogen while remaining net exothermic. Its approximate bookkeeping is $6p \rightarrow {}^4He + D + 3e^+ + 3\nu_e$, subject to verification of the full network, Q values, branchings, leakage, and temperature windows.

The strategic objective is deuterium output greater than D-T consumption for ignition and compression. The proposed neutron-producing step is [`13C(α,n)16O`](../reactions/c13-a-n-o16.md), followed by the separate neutron-capture/recovery step [`n(p,γ)D`](../reactions/n-p-g-d.md). The latter requires a hydrogen-rich capture mantle or recovery blanket and a neutron-balance calculation; it is not assumed to occur during the hot CNO burn. The return network is recorded in the linked reaction pages.
