# Staged CNO Hydrogen Burning

[← Fuel cycle](README.md) · [Study navigation](../README.md)

This pathway splits thermonuclear captures from slow beta decays, allowing radioactive intermediates to cool and wait outside the inertial-confinement interval.

Its simplified network restores the CNO catalyst and has the net reaction $4p \rightarrow {}^4He + 2e^+ + 2\nu_e + \text{energy}$. It produces no surplus deuterium, so its viability depends on negligible D-T use or an external breeder.

Relevant reaction records: [`14N(p,γ)15O`](../reactions/n14-p-g-o15.md), [`15N(p,α)12C`](../reactions/n15-p-a-c12.md), [`12C(p,γ)13N`](../reactions/c12-p-g-n13.md), and [`13N(p,γ)14O`](../reactions/n13-p-g-o14.md).
