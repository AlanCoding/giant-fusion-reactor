# Architecture

[← Study navigation](README.md)

The desired reactor scales primarily through fuel collection and target size: assemble a condensed target, compress it when necessary, ignite a local region, and allow the target to burn under inertial confinement.

The central distinction is between **collection-limited** scaling, where trigger mass becomes negligible compared with target mass as targets grow, and **machinery-limited** scaling, where each kilogram of fuel requires proportional active compression, confinement, or beam processing.

D-T used only for ignition and D-T used to supply compression energy are separate accounting cases. The latter cannot be assumed negligible at large scale.

The chamber, recovery buffers, and cryogenic handling are intentionally out of scope for the first numerical pass; the fuel target is the immediate object of study.
