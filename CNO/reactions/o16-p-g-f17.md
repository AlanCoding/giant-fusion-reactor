# 16O(p,γ)17F

[← Reaction records](README.md) · [D-breeder pathway](../fuel-cycle/deuterium-breeder.md)

**Role:** proposed return-network capture after the neutron-producing step.

## Required post-shot decay: $^{17}$F to $^{17}$O

$$
{}^{17}F\xrightarrow[T_{1/2}=64.385\ \mathrm{s}]{\beta^+\,/\,\epsilon}
{}^{17}O^{(*)}+e^++\nu_e.
$$

The evaluated decay overwhelmingly feeds stable $^{17}O$ in its ground state.
The evaluated upper limit for feeding the $871$-keV excited state is below
$0.034\%$; if populated, it promptly gamma-decays to the same stable isotope.
There is no material fractional branch to another daughter in this ledger.
$^{17}O$ is stable and remains available for the next loaded
$^{17}$O$(p,\alpha)^{14}$N reaction. The processing wait is about $3.6\ \mathrm{min}$
for $90\%$ decay or $7.1\ \mathrm{min}$ for $99\%$.

## Alternative hot pathways and leakage

The desired channel is $^{16}O(p,\gamma){}^{17}F$, which retains the catalyst in the documented return leg. Other proton-induced channels and excited-state branches must be evaluated across the actual implosion ion-energy distribution. A product that returns to $^{13}N$, $^{14}N$, $^{15}O$, $^{16}O$, or $^{17}O$ may be recoverable after decay/separation; any product outside that map is parasitic until a new return route is documented.
